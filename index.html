How it works and its limits

Everything runs in your browser. Messages are checked on this device, then forgotten. Nothing is saved, sent to a server, or reported to a parent.

It can miss things and can flag harmless messages. You can always choose "Send anyway." It helps your own judgment and doesn't replace talking to a trusted adult.

If something online feels scary or wrong, tell a parent, teacher, or another adult you trust.
